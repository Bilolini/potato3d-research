## Common geometry and transforms

Use the released segmented PLYs. Convert XYZ from meters to camera mm and project them back to the full-resolution frame using the released D405 intrinsics. Verify one-to-one pixel correspondence, supplied alpha membership, and agreement with raw millimeter depth. Allow at most 0.1 mm XYZ reconstruction discrepancy and 0.1 px rounding discrepancy. This defines common depth support for ICP, RAFT and final clouds. No support expansion beyond the release or learned masks. Consecutive saved frames estimate motion. Only lower, center and upper points may enter a fused output. A failed link makes that potato unscorable for the affected fusion method. Retain the center result and report the failed link without substituting frames.

Use 15 iterations each, correspondence gates of 12, 8 and 5 mm and retain the nearest 80% of in-gate matches. Fit with proper rotations and require at least 40 correspondences. The final link needs at least 30% source overlap within 5 mm. No reference inputs.

Use Torchvision RAFT-small with pretrained C_T_V2 weights (`raft_small_C_T_V2-01064c6d.pth`), CPU FP32, six threads and 12 update steps. Preserve RGB in a common union crop of the two foreground bounds, expanded by 24 pixels where possible and to at least 128 per dimension. Pad on the bottom and right to multiples of eight. ## Frozen endpoints

Use 50,000 uniform-by-triangle-area reference samples per potato with seed 5272401 and the same samples across methods. Coverage is the fraction within 2 mm of an output cloud. The second primary metric is the fraction of output points with exact point-to-triangle distance greater than 2 mm. For the density check, within each potato downsample without replacement to the smallest full output point count among the three methods. Average coverage over ten seeded repeats. Require all three outputs for this check.
