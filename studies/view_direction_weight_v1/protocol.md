# Development protocol for centroid-ray weight estimation

Test the centroid-ray variant of H1 from the experiment specification. Use exactly the 118 training and 40 validation potatoes in the archived three-frame weight study. Use all 3,368 archived training frames and only the 120 archived lower, center and upper spaced validation frames. A position name refers to the numerical suffix order. Retain the original exclusions. Check exact IDs against the scored SAM 2 confirmation roster and prior reserves, using roster metadata only. Open image and point-cloud payloads for the frozen train and validation frames only. Do not rotate clouds or fit PointRAFT.

Freeze this protocol and the exact PLY and full-resolution RGB(A) ZIP members before fitting. Join frames by exact tuber ID and numerical suffix, and require unambiguous members with matching checksums. Read the original provided alpha mask. Treat each nonzero pixel as equally weighted, including pixels with missing depth. Compute the centroid in original 1280 × 720 coordinates with zero-based column `u` and row `v`, before any cropping or cloud centering. Let `rx = (u − cx)/fx` and `ry = (v − cy)/fy` from the released D405 pinhole intrinsics. These are dimensionless camera-ray coordinates `X/Z` and `Y/Z`, with positive `rx` to image right and positive `ry` down. The forward ray is proportional to `[rx, ry, 1]`. Check the axis signs and projection round trip with known pixels and with the measured centroids. Record train and validation ranges, ranges by validation position, and within-potato spans across the three validation positions. Stop if a required frame, feature or target is unavailable. Do not substitute frames or change the cohort.

Reuse the archived geometry feature unchanged. Compute the camera-XY convex hull area in mm² from the released segmented PLY. Multiply by max(0, 350 mm − fifth-percentile camera depth in mm), then divide by 1000 for the bulk proxy in cm³. Use the released `weight_g_inctack` in grams, joined by exact tuber ID. Fit both models by weighted least squares on identical training frames. A frame for potato i has weight `1 / n_i` and each potato has total weight one. Model A has an intercept and bulk proxy. Model B has an intercept, bulk proxy, `rx`, `ry`, `proxy × rx`, and `proxy × ry`. Fit without validation-dependent preprocessing, feature selection, tuning or regularization. For both models floor predictions at zero grams, as in the archived estimator.

Save both train-only coefficient vectors before validation scoring. Require full-rank designs, Model A coefficients within 1e-9 in their respective units of the archived values, and Model A predictions within 1e-8 g of every archived selected-frame prediction. The comparison uses one score per potato. The primary endpoint averages the three absolute frame errors within each potato before averaging across potatoes. Report both MAEs and the paired B-minus-A difference. Negative favors B. Report the largest gain and loss by this endpoint, with ties ordered by tuber ID. For secondary comparisons report individual-position MAE and signed bias (prediction minus measured weight), and the absolute error of each model's arithmetic mean of its three predictions per potato. Report the primary and averaged-prediction paired differences with two-sided 95% percentile intervals using the same 10,000 paired tuber bootstrap draws, seed 5272401. Note that the 40 validation potatoes informed development decisions. The centroid ray measures image position relative to the camera axis. Report the observed variation and keep interpretation specific to this estimator and development cohort.
