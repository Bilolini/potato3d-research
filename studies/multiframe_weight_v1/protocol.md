# Three-frame conveyor weight development experiment

Compare center alone, the mean of three adjacent frame predictions and the mean of three spaced frame predictions. Use PointRAFT's public train IDs for fitting and val IDs for development. Keep PointRAFT test outcomes and geometry closed. Remove the release's non-perfect IDs, including the already documented conservative exclusion of 2R3-6 for 2R306. Exclude both earlier 36-object cohorts and the 29-object CoRe++ sensitivity cohort. Also leave out all original local potato demo and development IDs. Join measured weight by exact label. A target must be unique, finite and positive. Do not read or export test weights. The target is `weight_g_inctack`, measured grams including the tack as indicated by the release field name.

## Frame freeze

Freeze frame choices and estimator before computing weight errors. The PLY suffix is the annotated bounding-box center transformed to `image height − image y`. The field midpoint is 360 on the 720-pixel image. Sort available released suffixes numerically for each eligible validation tuber. Choose the suffix nearest 360 for center, then the next available on either side for adjacent. Choose the available suffix nearest each quarter position of the observed minimum-to-maximum suffix span for spaced, independently of the neighbors. Require the two quartile choices to strictly bracket center and both to lie farther out than the adjacent choices. Break distance ties toward lower suffix. Exclude invalid layouts before fit and record them. Use exactly three distinct frames for each three-frame condition and keep the same center and tuber denominator. Adjacency and spacing refer to released positions. No capture timestamps or view angles are assumed.

## Estimator

Fit one weighted least-squares regression with an intercept and one geometric bulk proxy, observed XY convex-hull area times robust height. Use all released frames for eligible train IDs, giving each tuber total weight one. The estimator uses supplied foreground PLYs derived from the release annotations. Load XYZ in camera meters and multiply by 1,000. Project onto the camera XY plane and compute convex-hull area in mm². Estimate height as the positive part of 350 mm minus the fifth percentile of camera depth, in mm. The fixed standoff is the prior project convention and approximate 2023 camera distance. Convert the product from mm³ to cm³ by dividing by 1,000. Predict grams as intercept plus slope times this proxy, floored at zero. Do not use reference dimensions, volume, cultivar or acquisition group as prediction features. A cloud must load, have finite XYZ and positive depths with median depth within 150–600 mm and positive projected area. Record invalid members without substituting frames. A validation member failure invalidates that potato for all three comparisons and stays visible in coverage.

## Summary

Compute each potato's arithmetic means, then absolute errors against the same measured mass. Primary paired difference is spaced minus adjacent error. Secondary comparisons are spaced minus center and adjacent minus center. Report the mean paired change and a two-sided 95% percentile interval with 10,000 paired tuber bootstrap draws seeded at 5272401. Negative means lower error. Ties are within 1e-9 g. Show all validation rows and highlight the largest primary improvement and worsening with individual frame estimates. Stop after the validation comparison.
