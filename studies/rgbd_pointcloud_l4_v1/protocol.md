# L4 center-frame RGB-D point-cloud development benchmark

## Frozen inputs

Use the exact 118 training and 40 previously studied development potatoes, center-frame members, provided alpha masks, D405 intrinsics and nominal 350 mm belt convention from [L3](../rgb_only_height_value_v1/frame_manifest.json). Check disjointness from the 45 SAM 2 confirmation potatoes and the original reserved rosters using metadata. Raw image payloads are limited to the frozen RGBA and raw depth members. Exclude caliper thickness, released segmented PLYs, SfM, and fused views.

Within the provided alpha mask retain D405 axial depths from 150 mm inclusive to 400 mm exclusive. Require at least 100 accepted pixels, at least half the silhouette and positive height. Compute height as 350 mm minus the fifth percentile over all valid pixels, before sampling. Replay the archived height, valid pixel count and support fraction by ID and center suffix. Back-project accepted pixels into XYZ in camera millimeters, attach their recorded RGB, and verify projection to the original pixels. No voxelization. Use a fixed uniform sample of 1,024 accepted pixels per potato without replacement, sorted by source index. Seed by the first eight bytes of SHA-256 of `rgbd-l4-sample-v1/` plus ID in big-endian order. Where fewer valid points remain than 1,024, keep all and append a seeded sample with replacement to reach 1,024. Mean-center sampled XYZ, divide by a fixed 100 mm to retain size, scale RGB by 1/255 and height by 1/100 mm.

## Model and training-only selection

Reuse the compact CPU PointRAFT-style network, inference and neighborhood builders from the archived [point-cloud development study](../raft_pointcloud_weight_v1/report.md). It uses two set abstractions with 64 and 16 deterministic farthest-point anchors and 16 neighbors at each level (radii 200 and 400 mm). The first shared MLP receives relative XYZ and three neighbor RGB channels, widening its first layer from three to six. Keep the remaining layers, pooling, height branch, LayerNorm, SiLU and head dropout 0.15. Train from scratch with no augmentation or pretrained weights. Weight in grams including the tack and water-displacement volume in mL use separate fits.

Take 24 inner-validation potatoes by SHA-256 order of `raft-pointcloud-inner-v1/133/` plus ID from the training pool. Fit each trait and seed on the remaining training IDs, using 100 epochs, batch 16, CPU FP32, four threads, Adam at 0.001 with weight decay 0.0001, cosine decay to 0.0001 over 100 epochs, and Smooth L1 with a 20-unit transition in each trait's physical units. Standardize the target using the fitting pool mean and population SD. Seeds are 133, 527 and 2401. For each trait and seed select the earliest epoch with minimum inner-validation MAE and refit through that epoch on all training potatoes, with the same schedule. Floor predictions at zero. The primary L4 prediction is the arithmetic mean of all three seed predictions.

## Evaluation

Write the L4 predictions and frozen fit receipts before opening any development weight or volume outcomes. Report MAEs for all five archived and current arms on the same complete cases per trait, with each arm's available coverage out of 40. Primary endpoint is L4 minus L3 paired absolute weight error. Repeat for volume. Use 10,000 paired whole-potato bootstrap draws, seed 5272401, and a two-sided percentile 95% interval. Record individual seed MAEs and changes, win/tie/loss counts, failures, and CPU timings. The comparison changes both input representation and estimator architecture.
