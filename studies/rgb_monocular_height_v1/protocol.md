Use the exact 40 development IDs and center PNGs of the [camera-height benchmark](../rgb_only_height_value_v1/protocol.md), with its supplied alpha masks, D405 intrinsics, fixed 350 mm belt scale and frozen weight and water-displacement-volume regressions. Use the official [Depth Anything V2 Metric Indoor Large HF checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Metric-Indoor-Large-hf/tree/d2fc6a93601aabb1139a3bf0ebfcb4e89c67817f), revision `d2fc6a93601aabb1139a3bf0ebfcb4e89c67817f`, SHA-256 `04d3295ff4f9cbee72b8998a85930fe870b45ba4431e7af47415fbded02388ca`. Run CPU FP32 in evaluation mode with PyTorch 2.7.1 and Transformers 4.53.2, six CPU threads, one image per forward pass. Use the checkpoint's DPT processor with whole-scene RGB, discarding alpha while retaining the original background colors. Use the released aspect-preserving resize, no pad, cubic image interpolation, rescaling by 1/255 and checkpoint mean/SD. Restore the metric predicted-depth map to the original 720 × 1280 mask grid with the processor's bicubic postprocessing. Use nominal metres, with larger values indicating farther points. Freeze the model, rules and inputs before the training checks or evaluation. Do not fit or calibrate to measured potato depths.

For the training-only checks, take the first three potatoes in SHA-256 order of `mono-l1-preflight-v1/` plus exact training ID, keeping one from each of the first three distinct acquisition groups. Check axis order and preprocessing/postprocessing alignment with synthetic RGB landmarks on the native grid, the model's metric configuration and depth range, and the relative foreground/background depth ordering on the fixed training images. Use matching training camera depth and the archived height only for direction and unit diagnostics. Compare the median camera and predicted depth across the target mask and a 20-pixel exterior ring, and replay the archived camera-height proxy. Save model, camera and RGB overlays with the target outline and pixels contributing to the upper background tail. Report any disagreement. Use synthetic checks and archived camera replay to guard against units/alignment mistakes. Do not alter the model, data or calibration rule based on the training comparison.

## Belt proxy and prospective predictions

Use valid predicted depth, defined as finite and strictly positive. Let `B` be the 85th percentile of every valid pixel outside the target alpha mask, including scene elements such as neighboring potatoes and roller gaps. Let `T` be the fifth percentile of valid pixels inside that mask. Calculate `h_mm = 350 × (1 − T/B)`. Save both quantiles in metres, valid pixel counts, the height, and per-frame runtime. The 85th percentile acts as a belt proxy. It may represent another part of the scene, and the model's foreground depth may be biased in the conveyor domain.

Invalid or missing quantiles, nonfinite quantities, and nonpositive heights make the L1 prediction unscorable and are counted separately. Keep every enrolled potato and record image/model failures. Use no imputation, truncation or substitute frame. Pass valid heights into the frozen L3 weight and volume regressions by replacing only the height in the shared area × height term. Keep the common projected area, aspect ratio and coefficient values, including the zero floor for traits. Save all 40 prediction records before opening the validation camera heights, L2 predictions or physical trait outcomes.

## Scoring

Compare L1 with the archived silhouette-only (L0), L2 RGB-predicted-height, and camera-height (L3) trait predictions. Score those four trait MAEs on the same complete potatoes per trait and report all arm-specific prediction and score coverage out of 40. For height, report L1 and L2 MAE and signed bias (prediction minus camera-height proxy) on the common scorable set. The primary endpoint is paired L1-minus-L0 absolute weight error in grams. Also report L1 minus L2 and L1 minus L3 for weight and volume. Negative differences favor L1. Use 10,000 paired whole-potato bootstrap draws with seed 5272401 and a percentile 95% interval. Report nonpositive or invalid estimates and development scope. Stop after this comparison.
