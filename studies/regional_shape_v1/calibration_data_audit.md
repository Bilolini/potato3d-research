# Can we independently locate the conveyor support surface?

**Audit closed.** I found no independent measurement of the physical support height at a tuber's RGB-D capture position in any of the 18 relevant acquisition groups. The calibration explanation for the confirmation result remains open. The scored cohorts stayed closed. [Research decision](support_offset_decision.md) · [Earlier calibration checks](../../docs/calibration_followup.md) · [Confirmation report](support_offset_confirmation_report.md)

## Acquisition and release coverage

The release's `ground_truth.csv` joined every study ID to its frozen acquisition group. Development covered 12 groups. The two 36-tuber cohorts each covered the same 15 Sayaka field and Corolle barn groups. The union includes 18 groups across the 2023 Sayaka field runs of September 14 and 21, the Kitahime barn runs of September 15, and the Corolle barn runs of September 22. PointRAFT's public metadata agreed for all 72 IDs in the two 36-tuber cohorts. For development IDs `2R2-6` and `2R3-10`, it interchanges the September 15 bag suffixes `_02` and `_03` relative to the local release. Both sources still assign 2023. That discrepancy would need to be resolved before linking those two frames to raw bags. The missing measurement is the **height of the supporting roller surface at the tuber's position**, in the RGB-D camera frame, with a bag-to-frame link and an uncertainty estimate. A same-session empty-conveyor depth sequence with a metric reference, or a survey of roller geometry and the camera pose tied to the recorded configuration, could supply it if roller position and variation under load are accounted for. The reviewed sources supply neither.
