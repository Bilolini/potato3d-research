## Endpoints

Use the pilot's mean symmetric closest-triangle distance with 20,000 deterministic area-weighted samples in each direction. Measure each edited mesh against its unchanged mesh in native TripoSR units. Per potato subtract the mean of the three peel displacements from the pin displacement. Separately subtract the maximum of the three peel displacements. An absolute contrast at most 1e-6 native units is a numerical tie. This tolerance exceeds the pilot's largest numerical self-distance. Apply the archived exact sign convention to the expanded-grid CoRe++ primary and strongest fixed peel medians, in mm. Keep effects in each model's own units. Classify all paired directions and retain failures in the ten-potato denominator. The comparison concerns response to each model's matched intervention on the same locations.
