# Fixed-weight regional reflection on fresh tubers

## Decision and population

Run one bounded object-level replication. The developmental leave-one-out gain was 0.099 mm and the median gain was 0.022 mm. Two tubers accounted for the aggregate gain. The 0.25 and 0.50 pooled errors differed by 0.000123 mm, so choose the smaller correction prospectively. The question is whether the fixed quarter-strength rule improves the error over the smooth fitted body across fresh measurable tubers. The seven evaluable development objects provide exploratory evidence only. [Weight study](reflection_weight_report.md)

The [fixed cohort](replication_cohort.json) contains 36 registered objects selected using metadata only (SHA-256 d9eb49dde8cc1111d564fcdcb528225d6d847d12205d33962b3d9fb69226a809). Exclude the five demo and twelve development IDs, the project's registration warning list, and rows outside the source experimental train/test labels. The latter restriction removes Kitahime from this replication. Use 24 Sayaka and 12 Corolle, taking one object per acquisition group per round within each cultivar. Sort groups and IDs by the hexadecimal SHA-256 of the UTF-8 prefix `regional-replication-v1/` plus the group name or object ID. Selection is independent by object from our development work. Acquisition groups and the source collection can be shared. Sample size is a feasibility budget. At the development coverage of 7/12 it would yield about 21 evaluable tubers, with substantial uncertainty.
