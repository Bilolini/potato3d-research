# CoRe++ pin recoloring and reconstruction accuracy on ten development potatoes

Use the ten registered development frames, their frozen original, pin and three peel inputs, and paired seeds 133–148. Reuse the archived 30³ grid and seed streams. Regenerate the decoded meshes from the verified interior SDFs and latents, and replay the added points at seeds 10133–10148. Check interior, outer and assembled SDF hashes, mesh volumes and paired edit displacements against the archive. Keep the 29 fresh test potatoes and both prior 36-potato cohorts closed. ## Reference and mapping gate

Audit 5R1-4 / 362 first, using the released preparation, loader and metric code. Recreate its loader target with the released 20,000-point uniform OBJ sampling at Open3D seed 133. Retain the original SfM axes and metric scale, and translate the OBJ by minus the sampled centroid exactly once. Preserve the same pose and scale for every seed and condition. Loader target points must match the released preparation program run with this seed within float32 serialization precision. Verify bidirectional transform consistency of named landmarks, the released RGB-D-to-SfM matrix applied to the archived scan and pin, and an overlay of the centered target with the unchanged decoded mesh. The RGB-D transform is a diagnostic only. Require a valid rigid transform, reasonable metric extents, pin-center agreement within 10 mm, and registered scan-to-SfM median and p95 distances at most 2 mm and 5 mm. Validate the coordinate mapping visually before freezing the references. A failed or unverifiable mapping stops the study before scoring.

The released test metric compares the decoded mesh directly to the loader target in centered SfM coordinates. For this study, retain the same fixed frame and measure symmetric mean **surface** distance. Sample 20,000 points from each triangle mesh with the archived deterministic, area-weighted sampler at seed 133, query exact closest points on opposing triangles via Open3D, and give each direction half weight. Report distances in mm. Check an identical triangular surface within 0.0001 mm and detect a 4 mm normal translation of that same surface within 0.0001 mm before scoring. Do not fit a separate rigid transform or scale for any condition.

Before reading any accuracy values from the initial run, a replay discrepancy on R12-7 established that equivalent Open3D meshes can enumerate triangles differently, changing which points the seeded sampler draws. The original attempt remains archived and separate. For the scored run, round coordinates to 13 decimal places in metres, sort vertices by XYZ, and sort the unordered vertex index triplets before sampling. The maximum coordinate rounding is 0.00000000005 mm. Verify exact metric invariance under changing vertex and face order and reversing face winding in the synthetic check. Require replayed SDF hashes and volume agreement to 0.00000001 mL. Report legacy sampled displacement discrepancies as a diagnostic, since the archive used the unsorted mesh order.

## Endpoints

Per seed report the unchanged, pin and all three peel errors. Define the first contrast as pin error minus unchanged error. Define the second as that pin change minus the mean of the three peel changes relative to unchanged. Positive values mean greater error. Take the median across the 16 paired seeds within each potato. Report all ten potatoes, the archived surface displacement alongside both accuracy contrasts, equal-potato means and medians, and positive/worsened, negative/improved and tied counts. A per-potato median within 0.0001 mm of zero counts as tied. Keep failures in the selected denominator and compute point estimates over complete potatoes. For the second contrast, negative means the pin performed better relative to the matched peel changes. The first contrast is needed to determine whether it improved on the unchanged prediction.
