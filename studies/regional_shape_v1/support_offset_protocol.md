# Can the depth image predict the underside offset?

On seven measurable development tubers, a shift estimated from reference heights in alternating sectors reduced underside MAE from 5.5121 to 3.7523 mm on the other sectors. The fitted shifts spanned −7.6264 to +5.4921 mm. This motivates testing whether an observable in the same frame predicts the signed correction. [Vertical-offset report](vertical_offset_report.md)

## One fixed development comparison

1. Use the original 12 development pairs and their registered RGB-D frames. Verify frame identity, depth units, and target masks. Reuse the existing background-plane estimator and its defaults in `src/potato3d/calibration.py`. The earlier demo audit shows why a plane fitted through visible conveyor rods is only a proxy for the surface supporting the tuber. [Calibration audit](../../docs/calibration_followup.md)
2. For each frame, predict one shift before reading reference heights. Evaluate the fitted background plane's camera depth at the scan-fitted footprint center in camera XY. In the archived nominal frame, height is 350 mm minus camera depth. Prespecify the additive underside shift as **350 mm minus the local plane depth** at that center. A local plane at 353 mm therefore predicts a −3 mm shift. Verify the camera Y convention and that a plane at 350 mm gives zero. This tests a rigid translation of the lower prediction, keeping the body fit, upper prediction, and scored cells fixed.
3. Save the input hashes, per-frame signed predictions, fit support, tilt, and failures before joining to the archived development outcomes. Use zero shift on fit failure and report how often that fallback was needed. Keep the 36 replication tubers closed. Use the jointly observed underside only for scoring and interpreting the comparison. Use the single fixed formula without learning a slope, intercept, or sign from these seven tubers.
4. Reproduce the 5.5121 mm body MAE using the archived masks and nested weighting on the seven measurable tubers. Compare each predicted shift with the archived best shift, then score the corrected underside on exactly the same cells. Report raw and shifted MAE, paired change per tuber, equal-tuber mean, and the mean change under each single-tuber omission. As a descriptive control, shuffle the same seven predicted shifts across tubers using all permutations. Report where the correctly matched mean MAE falls within that distribution. Acquisition groups can share imaging conditions, so this comparison is descriptive.

## Decision

Advance to a fresh-cohort protocol only if matching each shift to its own frame improves on both the unshifted body and the average shuffled assignment, and the gain over the body survives each single-tuber omission. Otherwise close this proxy as a candidate under the fixed rule. Report the magnitude in mm in either case. A poor result leaves both underside shape and the accuracy of the conveyor measurement open as explanations. Return the report for research review before reserving a fresh cohort.
