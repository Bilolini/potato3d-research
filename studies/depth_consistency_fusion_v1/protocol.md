# Depth-consistency filter for three-view potato fusion

## Frozen inputs

Use the ten geometry potatoes from the archived one-click [SAM 2 to RAFT study](../sam2_raft_end_to_end_v1/report.md), with its exact lower, center and upper frames, predicted masks, accepted pixels and frozen RAFT poses. Verify the masked depth support and unvoxelized center and fused clouds against the archived cache. Retain every center point before voxelization. Filter only raw points contributed by the two outer frames. The existing support depends on measured tuber thickness. No tracking, registration fitting, trait analysis or other cohort is opened.

## Reference-free point check

For each added point, project it into both other selected depth views using the archived source-to-center and inverse target-to-center poses and released intrinsics. Depth is axial camera Z in mm. Use the four pixel depths enclosing its projected position. A view gives evidence only if the projection is in front of the camera, the four pixels exist, and all four pixels passed the archived support rule. A point contradicts the view if its Z is more than the tolerance in front of even the closest of those four depths. Reject it if either other view supplies a contradiction. A point farther behind the measured depths may be occluded. Missing, unsupported or out-of-frame data give no evidence.

For each view, estimate local depth scatter from the center pixel minus the mean of its four cardinal neighbors, with all five pixels in the archived support. On a locally planar depth field this removes the first-order slope. Multiply the median absolute deviation about the residual median by 1.4826 and divide by sqrt(1.25) to estimate a per-pixel scale under independent residuals. Use a floor of 0.5 mm, half the released 1 mm depth step. For the source-to-target registration term, use the root sum of squares of the archived 3D inlier RMSEs along the adjacent links between those frames. The tolerance is twice the root sum of squares of the two depth scales and the registration term. This margin uses the 3D residual rather than only its axial component. The estimate assumes uncorrelated link errors and uses inlier fits, so it does not account for systematic drift or represent a calibrated probability bound. Compute and freeze the tolerances and rejection decisions using RGB-D and registration metadata only.

## Controls and scoring

Compare archived SAM 2 center, archived three-view SAM 2 fusion, depth-filtered fusion and count-matched random deletion. For each potato, make 20 uniform random draws without replacement of the same number of raw added points rejected by the filter, separately within each outer view, using NumPy PCG64 seed 5272401 plus the frozen roster index. Retain all center points. Freeze the rejection masks and random draws before opening the SfM references. Post-voxel point counts can differ because voxel occupancy depends on which points were removed.

After freezing, use the same released pose and 1 mm SfM-frame voxel centroids for all four arms. Replay the center and unfiltered archive scores. Use one identical set of 50,000 area-uniform reference samples per potato with the archived seed. Report reference coverage within 2 mm of a cloud and fraction of cloud points more than 2 mm from the mesh. For retention, take the reference samples covered by unfiltered fusion but missed by center. Report how many of these same samples each arm still covers, as a fraction of this original set and as estimated area in mm². Report total newly covered surface beyond center separately in case centroid movement covers different samples. Average random-repeat results within potato first and weight potatoes equally. Show all ten rows and report failures. Descriptive paired intervals use the archived 10,000 whole-potato percentile bootstrap draws, seed 5272401. If the mean retained share of the original new surface is below half, the filter primarily removed newly recovered coverage. Stop after this rule and its matched control, regardless of outcome.
