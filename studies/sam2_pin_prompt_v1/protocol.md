# Paired development protocol for moving a SAM 2 click from pin to peel

Use the same 40 development IDs, entry-to-center RGB sequences, CPU FP32 tracker settings, SAM 2.1 Hiera Tiny checkpoint, raw-depth filter with measured thickness, and frozen Model A as the center-weight study. Keep test and reserved cohorts closed. The released pin polygons and alpha masks simulate an operator who can identify nearby peel.

Join the COCO annotation by the exact relative RGB filename in the archived manifest. Treat a click on or inside a polygon as a pin click and rasterize polygon support with the release's OpenCV integer fill convention. For the audit and temporal classification, call a track a potato mask when IoU with the potato alpha is at least 0.5. Below that threshold, call a nonempty mask pin-dominant if more than half its predicted pixels lie on the released pin polygon. Record other and empty masks separately. Require the archived audit to reproduce the expected 12 inside-pin clicks with 12 pin-dominant center masks and 28 outside-pin clicks with four pin-dominant center masks before inference.

For each inside-pin original click, select one pixel inside the provided entry potato mask with Euclidean distance of at least 5 pixels to the nearest rasterized pin pixel and to the nearest non-potato pixel, counting outside the image as background. Minimize squared Euclidean distance from the original click. Break ties by smaller y and then smaller x. Freeze both coordinates, clearance, and movement before inference. Preserve the other 28 clicks and their archived scores for the paired result.

Replay the four outside-pin failures with their unchanged prompt. Compare the immediate click prediction and every propagated mask through center to that frame's potato alpha and pin polygon. Record both IoUs, the fraction of predicted pixels on the pin and the state. Report the first pin-dominant output and whether it was immediate. Require center pixel equality to the archived mask before running the 12 moved tracks.

The primary recovery criterion on the 12 changed tracks is an updated center potato IoU of at least 0.5. Unscorable masks remain in the recovery denominator. For weight, apply the exact archived raw-depth support and Model A. Keep every numeric estimate regardless of mask recovery. Report full-cohort MAE with unchanged archived predictions, its paired change from original one-click MAE, and its gap to provided-mask MAE. If a weight is missing, display full-cohort coverage and report the three directly comparable MAEs over the same scorable IDs. Do not tune the clearance, prompt, or scoring based on outcomes.
