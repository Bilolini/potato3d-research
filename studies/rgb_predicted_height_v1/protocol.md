# L2 height prediction from one center-frame RGB image

## Frozen inputs

Use the exact 118 training and 40 development potatoes, provided alpha masks, original 1280 × 720 center PNGs, released D405 intrinsics and nominal 350 mm scale from the [camera-height benchmark](../rgb_only_height_value_v1/protocol.md). Verify disjointness from the 45 SAM 2 confirmation IDs and reserved rosters using metadata. The 40 development cases have already been studied. The only image payload opened in this study is the frozen center RGBA. Keep its native pixel scale. No colors outside the provided alpha mask are used.

Train against the archived camera-height proxy in mm, defined as 350 mm minus the fifth percentile of accepted raw axial depth inside the provided mask under the archived range and support rules. Fit and check the two predictors using training heights only. The validation depth-derived height and trait values stay closed until the frozen validation predictions are saved.

## Compact height predictors and model selection

Use the same standardized ridge regression form for both predictors, with an unpenalized intercept. The silhouette predictor takes three features: square root of the archived projected area in mm, archived log major-to-minor axis ratio, and silhouette area divided by the convex hull area of the foreground pixel footprint. The RGB predictor retains those three terms and adds five appearance features. Convert recorded RGB values to [0,1], use foreground only, and compute the median and 90th-minus-10th percentile of luma, plus median red and green chromaticity (channel divided by the sum of RGB, defined as zero for black pixels). Use luma weights 0.2126, 0.7152 and 0.0722 on the recorded channel values. The fifth appearance feature is the median luma of the deepest quartile of foreground pixels by distance inside the silhouette minus median luma of the nearest quartile. Compute the distance in original pixel units to the mask boundary, with a one-pixel background border. Quartile ties are included. Both predictors retain their native apparent size through the shared projected area. Color is summarized without extra annotation or pretrained features.

Choose ridge penalties independently using the same leave-one-acquisition-group-out folds among the 118 training potatoes. Fit scaling within each fitting fold. Use `mean squared height residual + lambda × sum of squared standardized coefficients` for penalties 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1 and 10. Use the lowest mean absolute out-of-fold height error across individual training potatoes, breaking ties within 1e-12 mm toward the larger penalty. Refit using all matching training cases. Floor height predictions at zero during selection and inference. Keep identical training rows for both height predictors. Save training choices and fitted coefficients and freeze validation features, heights and substituted trait predictions before joining validation heights or physical traits. Missing inputs stay visible.

## Trait substitution and endpoints

Keep both archived trait regressions and their scale fixed. In the depth-assisted regression, replace only `A × camera_height / 1000` by `A × predicted_height / 1000`, where `A` is the same projected mask area in mm². Keep the zero prediction floor. Apply it separately to the archived weight and water-displacement-volume fits with the two predicted heights. Replay the archived silhouette-only trait baseline and archived measured-height trait predictions before comparing the four arms. These reference predictions use the same historical height proxy and trait measurements as the previous benchmark.

The primary endpoint is RGB-plus-silhouette predicted-height weight error minus archived silhouette-only trait error in grams. For each trait also report RGB minus silhouette-predicted height, RGB minus camera height and camera height minus the silhouette-only trait baseline. Report the weight advantage recovered as mask-only MAE minus RGB-predicted-height MAE, and the value of appearance as silhouette-predicted-height MAE minus RGB-predicted-height MAE, with signed differences and paired intervals. The four trait MAEs use the same complete cases, and the enrolled denominator stays 40 for coverage reporting. Score height MAE, signed bias (prediction minus camera proxy) and the paired RGB-minus-silhouette absolute height error on common scorable cases.

Use a percentile 95% interval with 10,000 paired whole-potato bootstrap draws, seed 5272401, and a tie tolerance of 1e-9 outcome units. Do not select features or settings on the development cases. Stop after this comparison.
